Ask on WhatsApp
What maintenance services do you provide?

We provide electrical, plumbing, AC & HVAC, painting and handyman services, together with preventive maintenance, property and building maintenance, move-in/move-out inspections and emergency maintenance.

Do you provide emergency maintenance?

Yes. For urgent problems such as leaks, power failures or AC breakdowns, WhatsApp us directly and our team will arrange help as quickly as possible.

Are your services available 24/7?

Yes, our maintenance support is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Non-urgent work is scheduled at a time that suits you.

Do you provide preventive maintenance?

Yes. We offer planned preventive maintenance for AC, electrical, plumbing and general building elements, and preventive visits are included in our packages.

Do you work on homes and commercial properties?

Yes. We maintain villas, apartments and townhouses, as well as offices, retail units, commercial premises and buildings.

How can I request a quote?

Fill in the quote form on this page — it opens WhatsApp with your details ready to send — or message us on WhatsApp directly. Include photos and a short description so we can understand the job. There is no online payment or booking; our team contacts you to confirm the details.

Do you provide maintenance packages?

Yes. We offer Bronze, Silver and Gold maintenance packages with different levels of spare parts coverage, labour and maintenance hours. Contact us for a package quotation.

How quickly can you respond?

We aim to respond to every request promptly. Response times depend on the nature of the issue and location; emergencies are prioritised.
